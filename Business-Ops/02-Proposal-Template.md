# Proposal Template

Rules before sending any proposal:
- Every claim must be true. No invented past clients, years of experience, or fake portfolio pieces.
- No guaranteed outcomes (e.g. "will increase your sales by X%").
- Must be reviewed/approved by owner before sending — this file is a draft generator, not an auto-send tool.

---

Hi [Name],

I noticed [specific detail about their business/post — proves you read it, not a template blast].

[One sentence: the specific problem you can see, e.g. "your current site isn't mobile-friendly" or "you don't have a landing page for X offer yet."]

I build fast, clean [websites/landing pages] using modern AI-assisted tooling, which lets me turn around a first draft in [realistic timeframe, e.g. 3-5 days] at [price/range]. [If no formal portfolio yet: "I'm building my portfolio right now, so I can offer [X] at a lower introductory rate in exchange for a review/case study."]

If useful, I can put together a quick mockup of your homepage before you commit to anything — no obligation.

Would [specific day/time or "this week"] work for a quick chat?

[Name]
[Portfolio link once it exists]

---
## Variants to keep on file
- Cold outreach (short, problem-first)
- Upwork/Fiverr proposal (platform-specific, references their exact posted brief)
- Warm/referral follow-up
