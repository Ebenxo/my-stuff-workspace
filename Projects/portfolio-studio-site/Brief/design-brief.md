# Design Brief - Studio Portfolio (form & flow)

Date: 2026-09-21. Requested by owner: "a befitting portfolio to match high ranking world top people in that space."

## What this is
The studio's own portfolio: a home page plus three case-study pages, each with a working copy of the sample it describes. Separate from `D:\my stuff\FormAndFlow\` (Codex's concept site, navy/lime) which is not touched. Uses the same working studio name and contact email already public on that site.

## Bar
Top freelance portfolios win on argued work, not effects: a clear point of view, few projects shown well, honest process. So: 3 case studies (research, decisions, what was left out, what would be measured, what is unfinished), and a "what I will not ship" standards section drawn from the owner's anti-generic checklist.

## Must-avoid
No invented clients, reviews, awards, stats or logos. No fake forms. All samples labelled as fictional. No claim of outcomes. Not the navy/lime, cream/terracotta or green looks used elsewhere.

## Deliberate omission
Bright Path Dental is NOT featured: its demo contains invented stats (4.9/5, 1,200+ patients, 70% less radiation) and emoji icons, which fail the owner's own checklist. Rebuild it to the Halloway standard before showing it.

## Format
Static HTML/CSS, no JS, no framework. `build.py` regenerates the pages from one template (run with Python, output to Drafts).
