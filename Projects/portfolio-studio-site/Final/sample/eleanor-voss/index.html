Editor & Researcher

Eleanor Voss

I work at the intersection of long-form writing and archival research, spending most of my time helping institutions turn dense material into something a reader can actually follow. Before that, I spent six years editing nonfiction for independent presses.

My interest is in what a document leaves unsaid: the context around a sentence, the history behind a name, and the details that make a story worth reading.
