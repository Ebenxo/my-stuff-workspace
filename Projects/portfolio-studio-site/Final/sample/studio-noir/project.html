Heron & Co. — Brand Identity

ClientHeron & Co. (fictional)
RoleBrand identity, logo, guidelines
Year2026

Heron & Co. needed a mark and a small system that could flex across packaging, signage, and a simple site — without needing a designer on call for every new use case.

The brief called for something quiet and confident rather than loud: a wordmark, a single supporting motif, a restrained two-color palette, and clear rules for spacing and minimum size so the identity would hold up at both business-card and storefront scale.

Deliverables included the primary and secondary logo lockups, a one-page usage guide, and packaging templates ready to hand to a printer.
