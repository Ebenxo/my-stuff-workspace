# Design Brief - Residential plumbing sample (US)

Date: 2026-09-21. Requested by owner (approved plumbing as the next vertical; also to serve the US-first outreach plan in Business-Ops/08-First-90-Days-Plan.md and research/global-from-lagos.md).

## What
A single-page sample site for a FICTIONAL owner-operated residential plumbing company (1-5 trucks) in Texas. Not a real business. Built from Business-Ops/research/plumbing.md.

## Verified by the director before building (2026-09-21)
- Gas smell (Wisconsin PSC, primary source): "leave the area immediately"; "from a safe distance outdoors, call the local gas utility's emergency number or 911"; "Do not use cell phones, lights, appliances, cars, flames, or sparks near suspected gas leaks". Publish only this shape. Do not add "open windows". Do not add DIY gas repair steps.
- Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners (primary source): the RMP licence number "should be displayed in all advertisements"; written estimates and invoices "should contain" the RMP licence number and the board's name, address and telephone. Use the word "should", not "must".

## Rules for this build
- Prices: sample fees must sit inside the sourced bands in the research file and be labelled as sample fees. Service-call fee $75-150 typical; state the exact credit rule.
- No real-looking licence number: use an obvious placeholder such as "RMP licence no. [client to supply]".
- No claims of licensed, insured, bonded, 24/7, free estimates or no hidden fees unless the sample explicitly defines them and marks them as the fictional firm's policy.
- Skip: frozen-pipe instructions, DOE-attributed lifespan figures, and any research item tagged SEARCH-ONLY without a second source. Use READ-tagged ranges labelled "typical".
- No testimonials, ratings, counts, logos, awards, stock people, emoji icons or fake forms. Phone/text links only.
- Distinct look: must not reuse the palettes or type of the other samples (charcoal/amber/Barlow; pine/paper/Newsreader; bone/cobalt/Bricolage; paper-navy/Source Serif; Codex site navy/lime/Arial). Avoid the plumbing cliches (blue water drop, wrench logo).

## Format
Single page, hand-written HTML/CSS, no JavaScript unless it does real work, in Drafts/. Never Final.
