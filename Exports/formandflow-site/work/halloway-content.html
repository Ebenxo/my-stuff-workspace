The brief

Turn Halloway’s repair offer into one week of useful content. Connect practical pre-enquiry questions to the service page. The calendar and editing brief below are concept deliverables; no published campaign is implied.

Sample editorial plan
MON · EXPLAINER POST

What the diagnostic visit includes

Save / visit the service page.

WED · SHORT VIDEO

How the enquiry process works

View the service scope.

FRI · QUESTION POST

What to prepare before contacting us

Read the guide / ask a question.

Video and audio editing brief

30-second edit plan: 0–4s, “Before you book a repair”; 4–20s, appliance type, fault and model number; 20–30s, review service scope and contact. Trim pauses, balance speech, check captions and use cleared footage and music.

Publishing and community

Example reply: “Which appliance is affected? Please send the model and fault details privately.” Log the question, owner and next action. Escalate complaints or safety concerns; do not promise a diagnosis or repair outcome in comments.

Production checklist

Clear speech, consistent audio, accurate captions, approved visuals, correct aspect ratio and a working destination link. This is an editing specification; a finished video or audio sample is not included.
