Product direction

An institution-focused attendance platform structured around administrators, teachers, students and parents. The design connects attendance capture with records, visibility, reporting and notifications, with a different information priority for each role.

Four user groups · product structure

Administrators

Institution records, attendance analytics and reporting.

Teachers

Class dashboards and attendance capture.

Students

Attendance records and relevant updates.

Parents

Visibility into attendance and notifications.

Design contribution

Product concept, information architecture, user roles and flows, dashboard structure, mobile navigation, feature architecture, wireframes, UI direction and roadmap.

Core experience

The product scope includes real-time attendance, student records, teacher dashboards, parent visibility, attendance analytics, reports, notifications and data exports.

Structure sketch
A structure sketch (side navigation, a top bar, summary cards and an attendance table), not an application screenshot or a claim of a live deployment.
Tools and technology

Figma, React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Zustand and API architecture.
