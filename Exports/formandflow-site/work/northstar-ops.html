Reporting and team rhythm

Maintain a weekly report covering lead sources, qualification, calls, wins, outstanding actions and data gaps. Separate facts from interpretation. Use brief handovers with an owner, deadline and blocker; record decisions where the team can find them.

Automation tool learning

Start with one use case and success criteria. Read the tool documentation, build a small test flow, exercise failure paths and compare the result with manual work. Document setup, limitations and recovery before extending the system.

Simulated delivery conditions

Exercise: an incomplete lead arrives twice while the coordinator is unavailable. Identify the duplicate, preserve the original context, assign a backup owner and log the next action. Assess record quality, escalation and handover clarity.
