Concept only, no live campaign
The brief

A small office-cleaning business wants more suitable enquiries without overwhelming its coordinator. The task: develop a campaign concept connecting a clear offer, a landing page, lead qualification and a simple follow-up process. Northstar Office Care is fictional.

Offer and brand

Audience: office managers seeking recurring cleaning. Concept message: “A cleaning plan that fits your office.” The page explains service scope, asks for location and frequency, and directs visitors to request a scoped quote.

Lead generation and sales

Every lead record carries: source, business, location, service need, timing, owner and next action. Qualification checks service area and recurring-service fit. Suggested reply: “Which days and areas need cleaning? We can use that to scope the next step.”

Illustrative funnel · synthetic data

Weekly execution

Review source quality and the oldest unanswered leads. If qualified prospects do not reach a call, inspect response time and scheduling friction before increasing lead volume.

Assessment and deliverables

Concept deliverables shown here: audience and offer, lead-field specification, initial reply and funnel example. A live implementation would add source tracking, a tested intake destination and a weekly pipeline review.

Commercial results would require a live campaign, agreed definitions and measured baseline data.
