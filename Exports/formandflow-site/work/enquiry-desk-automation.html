The brief

The working browser prototype — the Enquiry Desk — already links a customer form with an owner review view, an enquiry status and a reply template, in the browser only. This page takes that prototype and asks what it would take to adapt it for a real fictional business, Northstar Office Care. The WordPress, AI and CRM connections below are an integration concept, not a deployed service.

WordPress and AI · concept architecture
01Capture

WordPress landing page and labelled enquiry form.

02Validate

Check required fields and reject duplicate submissions.

03Route

Create a lead record and notify the assigned owner.

04Assist

AI suggests a summary and reply for human review.

Failures and human review

Keep form input when submission fails. Show success only after the destination confirms receipt. Log delivery failures for retry. Treat AI text as a draft; a person approves facts, tone and any message sent.

Integration acceptance checks

Test a valid enquiry, missing fields, a duplicate, a failed destination and an unavailable AI service. Keep credentials out of page code. Confirm data destination, permissions and retention before launch.

Further scope

Additional automation concepts: WhatsApp support, FAQ replies, appointment flows, qualification and CRM handoff. Technology scope: Python, Node.js, OpenAI APIs, LangChain, REST APIs, webhooks and local LLMs. These are concepts, not claimed live deployments.
