Dream Vision

Bold identity. Clear shopping. A streetwear identity exploring a youthful, premium visual language alongside a clean digital shopping experience. The work connects brand direction with a landing-page concept and product presentation.

Identity scope

Brand direction, typography, colour system and art direction. The aim is to establish a recognisable visual language that carries across the brand’s touchpoints.

Digital scope

Landing-page concept, product presentation, mobile design and digital brand consistency. Skills applied: branding, art direction, UI design, web design and visual systems.

Fanny’s Inn

An identity for hospitality. A restaurant branding exploration combining elegance and contemporary hospitality. The supplied direction pairs charcoal, warm ivory, muted gold and rich accents with editorial typography.

Brand development

Logo exploration, monogram concepts, emblem and crest directions, a colour system, typography, menu direction, packaging concepts and the restaurant’s broader visual language.
