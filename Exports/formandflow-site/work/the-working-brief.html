Keep the voice. Sharpen the story.

Type
Podcast & video editing concept

Fictional interview podcast for founders and creative operators

Editing concept, no recorded episode claimed
The fictional client brief

Turn a 40-minute, two-camera interview into a focused episode and three short promotional clips. Preserve the speaker’s meaning, remove distractions and create versions that are easy to follow with or without sound.

Episode structure · proposed edit
OPENStrong moment

A hook pulled from later in the conversation.

INTROWho and why

Guest, subject and why this episode.

COREThree themes

The main interview, structured around three ideas.

CLOSEKey takeaway

One idea to leave the listener with.

Picture and story

Sync camera angles and separate audio. Build a selects list before cutting; remove repetitions without changing meaning. Cut between speakers deliberately, match exposure and colour, then add restrained titles and checked captions.

Voice and sound

Reduce steady noise conservatively, trim distracting pauses and balance speaker levels. Check edits for clicks, clipping and audible pumping. Keep music beneath speech and use only approved, licensed audio.

Delivery package

One full-length 16:9 video, an audio-only episode, three captioned 9:16 clips, an SRT caption file, a thumbnail and timestamped show notes. Proposed tool options: DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Pro, with Audacity for audio cleanup.

Final review

Check lip sync, caption names, safe text placement, colour continuity and audio on headphones and speakers. Confirm platform export requirements. Collect timecoded feedback, version the revisions and archive source files with the final exports.

Concept deliverables and tool options are presented as an editing plan, not evidence of completed client work or software proficiency.
