What we fix

Appliances we service

If it's not on this list, we'll tell you honestly rather than take the call anyway — see what we don't service further down.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Not cooling, freezer frosting up, warm fridge/cold freezer imbalance, unusual compressor noise, water leaking from the base.

Washers

Won't drain or spin, leaking from the door or base, loud banging on spin cycle, error codes, won't start at all.

Dryers

Not heating, drum turning but no heat, taking two-plus cycles to dry, squealing belt, drum not turning.

Dishwashers

Not draining, dishes coming out dirty or cloudy, leaking onto the floor, won't start, error codes.

Ovens & Ranges

Won't reach temperature, igniter clicking but not lighting (gas), burner won't light, self-clean cycle not completing.

Built-in Microwaves

Not heating, turntable not turning, door latch failure, display dead, tripping the breaker.

Ice Makers

Not making ice, making ice but not dispensing, small or hollow cubes, ice tasting off (usually a line or filter issue).

Garbage Disposals

Humming but not spinning (usually a jam), leaking from the bottom, tripped reset button, unusually loud grinding.

Honest advice

Is it worth repairing, or should you replace it?

We'll tell you straight — sometimes the honest answer is "buy a new one," and we'd rather say that at the diagnostic stage than take a repair fee for something that won't be worth it in a year.

The 50% rule we actually use

If the repair costs more than 50% of a comparable new unit, and the appliance has already passed roughly half its expected lifespan — replacing usually makes more financial sense than repairing. If only one of those is true, repair is usually still the better call.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask before booking

My appliance is 11 years old — is it even worth calling you?

Often, yes. Age alone doesn't decide it — see the Repair or Replace section above. A lot of 10+ year old machines just need a $40-60 part, which is well under the 50% threshold. We'll tell you honestly at the diagnostic if yours isn't one of those.

Do you carry parts on the truck, or do I have to wait for an order?

The truck stocks the parts that fail most often (thermostats, door seals, drain pumps, heating elements, belts, igniters), so most repairs finish same-visit. Less common parts get ordered and we're usually back within 2-3 business days.

What if you get here and can't fix it same visit?

You still pay the $79 diagnostic fee, which is credited toward the repair once we come back to finish it. You won't pay it twice.

Do you work with landlords and rental properties?

Yes — we can invoice a property manager or landlord directly if the tenant is the one home for the appointment. Just let us know when you book.

How do I pay?

Card, check, or cash at the time of service. No online payment portal — you pay the technician directly when the work is done.
