How we decide on X-rays

Not on a calendar. National guidance from the ADA and FDA is that X-rays should follow your individual risk, not a fixed schedule. For an adult with healthy teeth and no history of decay, bitewings are typically needed only every two to three years. If you have had many cavities, we will suggest them more often, roughly every six to twelve months.

If you bring recent X-rays from another dentist, we will use them rather than repeat them.

Only if needed

Full-mouth X-ray series: $165. Panoramic X-ray: $130. We take these when there is a clinical reason, such as planning an extraction or a crown, and we will say why before we take them.

Anything beyond the exam is quoted first. You approve the plan and the price before treatment starts.

Pain or injury

Is it an emergency? A plain guide

Call us first. We would rather tell you honestly whether it can wait than have you sit in a waiting room, or in an emergency room you did not need.

Call now, do not wait until morning

  • A permanent tooth has been knocked out
  • Swelling of the face or gums, especially with fever
  • Severe or constant pain that medicine is not controlling
  • Bleeding that will not stop

Go to a hospital emergency room instead if swelling makes it hard to swallow or breathe, or after significant injury to the jaw or face.

If a permanent tooth is knocked out

Time matters: the ADA says the tooth has the best chance of being saved within about 30 minutes.

  1. Pick it up by the crown, not the root, and do not scrub it.
  2. If you can, gently place it back in the socket. If not, keep it moist in milk or between your cheek and gum. Do not let it dry out.
  3. Call us on the way: (555) 018-3344.

A mild toothache with no swelling or fever can usually wait for the next available appointment. Call and we will say when. This guide is general information, not a diagnosis.

Insurance and payment

How payment works here

With insurance

We are not in network with any plan, so we bill you at our fee and file the claim for you. Your plan reimburses you or us according to its out-of-network rules. Ask your plan what it pays out of network before you book, and we will give you a written estimate of our fees for any procedure.

Without insurance: the membership

$299 a year

Two exams, two standard cleanings and the bitewings you need in the year, plus 15% off other treatment. It costs less than paying per visit if you would come twice anyway. If you would come once, do not join.

Payment is by card or check at the visit. Missed appointments: please give 24 hours’ notice. We do not charge the first one.

What we treat

What we do, and what we send elsewhere

  • We do: exams, cleanings, fillings, crowns, simple extractions, night guards, gum-disease treatment, emergency pain relief and repairs.
  • We refer out: braces and aligners, dental implant placement, wisdom-tooth surgery, root canals on back molars, and children under four. Each goes to a specialist we know, with your records sent ahead.
  • We do not offer: sedation beyond local anaesthetic, or cosmetic treatment we would not recommend for your teeth.

Questions

What people ask before booking

I am nervous about the dentist. Can we go slowly?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we will set the first visit for exam and conversation only, and stop whenever you ask. Nothing is done without you saying yes to it.

Do I really need X-rays every visit?

No. We decide from your history and what we see, not from the calendar. See the section above.

Can I get a written estimate before I commit?

Yes, for any procedure, before it is scheduled. The estimate lists our fee, and the number of visits.

What if I cannot afford the treatment you recommend?

Tell us. We will explain which parts are urgent and which can wait, and what the cheapest sound option is. You decide.
